Round 3: The rest of the app

Every page, same system

Every page uses the shell from 2a and the same four parts: a page header with one main action, views with counts, a short table with a More columns option (nothing removed, just tucked away), and a record panel that opens with Next step. Click rows, views and buttons to try them. The sidebar links go between pages.

3a
Purchase orders
Views like "Needs action" instead of a raw status column. A 6-phase progress bar with a plain-language next step and who it's waiting on. Open any order to follow a checklist and move it on with one button. "New purchase order" is a 6-step guided form.
3b
Inventory
Status tiles double as filters. Each SKU shows a stock runway (days left, plus when restock lands), so gaps show up visually. Open a SKU for the forecast: a plain-English headline, one action, a 60-day stock chart, and "How is this worked out?"
3c
Products
A List or Board view, both by lifecycle stage, with approval chips and a "Needs" column. The record opens with Right now, approvals and Notes (with a Team only / Partner can see toggle), then details.
3d
Shipments
Views: Late, Arriving this week, Further out, Delivered. Each row shows a journey track and a plain-language arrival time. The record starts with what to do.
3e
Approvals
A queue plus a review pane: photos, the factory's note, a checklist, and three big decision buttons. Asking for changes or rejecting needs a comment. A History tab keeps the record.
3f
Invoices
Totals double as filters. "Record payment" sits right on each row, and a Billed to partners tab covers reimbursements. "Add invoice" is 4 steps: upload the PDF, match it to a PO, check the details, save.
Round 2: Home

A Home you build from widgets

Every part of 1a, 1b and 1c is now a widget. Saved views sit in the tabs (CEO overview, Operations, Finance). Click Customize this view to drag widgets around, pick a preset size, open Settings for each widget's filters, or add widgets from the library. New view starts from a role template or from blank.

Round 1: Home

Three ways Home could work

All three share the same new sidebar and header. Each one is shown in dark and light. Use the Light/Dark button in any header to switch it live, and click rows, stages or tasks to try them. Use the role tweak to switch between Ops, Finance and Partner.

1a
Today: a to-do list first
Home opens with the jobs that need you, most urgent first. Each one has a single clear button. Click a row to see why it matters and which step it's on. Quick actions for your most common jobs sit on top. The best fit for new or less technical teammates.
1b
Board: see the whole operation
Charts first. The product pipeline shows every stage and how many products are stuck. Click a stage to see what's in it. Stock is drawn as a runway (days left, and when the restock lands), so a stockout gap shows up without any math. The best fit for managers who want the big picture.
1c
This week: organized by time
Three plain-language headlines, then a calendar of what lands, what's due and what launches each day. Below that, "Your next step" walks one record through its checklist. The best fit if timing is where things slip.
